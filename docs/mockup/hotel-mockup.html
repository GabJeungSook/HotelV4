The whole app, screen by screen

Mockup v6 · operations look, people and timelines, new sign-in and welcome · 16 November 2026

Homi is the rebuild of HotelV2: a general system for small hotels, inns and pension houses that sell rooms by the hour, overnight, or both, under one owner with one or more branches. Front desk staff use it on a desktop browser, guests on a portrait kiosk, housekeepers on their phones, and back office and admin on desktop. Three rules drive every screen: money is written once to a ledger and never edited, a shift is a real record that every peso points to, and a room can only have one open stay. What used to be fixed for one hotel is now a branch setting (46). The sample hotel "Hotel Dalisay", its people and its prices are made up; the logo and the blue are the real Homi brand.

1Sign in and open the shift

Signing in and running a shift are now two separate things. Logging out, closing the browser or a session timeout never closes a shift or touches the cash.

2Room board

The front desk's home. Every room is one tile, and the tile's colour comes from the room's status plus its open stay, so the board cannot show a room as free while someone is still checked in.

3Check-in

One screen, one Save. The price comes from the rate table and cannot be typed. Saving writes the stay, the room charge, the deposit and the payment together, or nothing at all.

4Guest folio

One screen per stay replaces V2's two guest screens and 14 pop-ups. The folio is a list of lines that are only ever added. A mistake is fixed by a reversing line with a reason, so the history always adds up.

5Check-out

A three-step guide that will not finish until the balance is zero and the deposit is settled. The guest no longer has to check out at the kiosk first.

6POS and stock

One product list for the branch. A sale is either paid in cash by a walk-in or charged to an occupied room, and both reduce the same stock.

7Cash during the shift

Money leaving or entering the drawer outside of guest payments: petty-cash expenses, cash sent to the owner, and staff paying back an earlier shortage.

8Close the shift and hand over

The part V2 got most wrong. Closing is a guided handover between two teams: check what is pending, count the cash without seeing the target, see the result, and have the incoming front desk accept the drawer.

9Kiosk

An optional module, switched on per branch (46). A portrait touchscreen in the lobby, running in a browser in full-screen mode. The guest picks a room and gets a code; payment always happens at the front desk. Kiosk check-out is removed.

10Housekeeping

Used on the housekeeper's own phone in the browser. One list, one big button. The V2 queue rules are kept: oldest check-out first, and finish what you started.

11Reports

Every report reads the same ledger through one report service, so they cannot disagree. A day is the AM shift plus the PM shift that started on that date; a month is the sum of its days.

12Admin

Setup for one branch, plus that branch's reports (36 to 43) in the same menu. An owner can open any branch from the branches list (55) and sees these same screens for it.

13Problems and edge cases

What staff see when something goes wrong. Each message says what happened and what to do next, and none of them leaves half-saved data behind.

14Owner and branches

The level above a branch. An owner account manages the list of branches, opens any of them as its admin, and reads every report for one branch or all together (39).

15Reservations

An optional module for branches that take advance bookings, mostly for overnight stays. It is a list and a form for the front desk; there is no online booking in version 1.

16Welcome, dashboards and stats

The public front page and the home screens of each role. The structure follows our Euphoria system; the look is Homi's own: warm neutral greys, thin borders instead of shadows, no gradients, one calm number per card, statuses as small soft labels, and every figure labelled with the period it covers. Dashboards read summary tables that are rebuilt every minute, load card by card, and refresh themselves every 30 seconds.

Building blocks

The shared parts every screen is built from. They are built on Nuxt UI v4 (buttons, inputs, modals, toasts, dropdowns), themed once with the Homi tokens, with our own components on top for what is special to a hotel: room tiles, money input, PIN pad, the stat card, and the chart kit. A button or a peso amount looks and behaves the same on every screen.
