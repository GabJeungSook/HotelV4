# Housekeeper — quick guide

One page, for your phone. Words in **bold** are the buttons on the screen.

## Sign in
- Open Homi on your phone and sign in with your own username and password. You see **Queue**: the rooms waiting to be cleaned, the most urgent first.

## Clean a room
1. Find the room at the top of the queue → **Start cleaning**. Only the first rooms in line can be started; the others say **Next in line**.
2. You can clean one room at a time. Finish it before you start another.
3. The timer runs while you clean. Tick each item on the checklist as you go.
4. When the room is ready for a guest → **Room is ready**. The front desk sees it as **Vacant** straight away.

## Something wrong in the room
- Broken aircon, leaking tap, a stain that will not come out: **Report a problem in this room**, write what is wrong under "What is wrong", then **Send to maintenance**. The room is blocked until it is fixed; you do not need to finish it.
- A guest is still in the room: **Room is ready** is refused. Tell the front desk.

## Late rooms
- A room shows **Late** when it was not ready before its deadline. Do the red ones first.

## Your day
- **My cleans** lists the rooms you finished today, your average time and how many were late.
- **Sign out** at the bottom when you finish.

## When something goes wrong
- **Red bar at the top:** the phone lost its connection. Wait until it goes away before you press anything.
- **"Something went wrong. Nothing was saved."** Try once more. If it happens again, write down the request id and tell the front desk or your admin.
