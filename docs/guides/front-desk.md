# Front desk — quick guide

One page. Print it and keep it at the desk. Words in **bold** are the buttons on the screen.

## Start of your shift
1. Sign in with your own username and password. Never use someone else's account.
2. **No shift open yet:** count the drawer, type the amount under "I counted the drawer and it has", then **Open shift**. If it is not what the last shift handed over, write what you found in the note.
3. **A shift is already open:** you see the drawer your colleague is holding. Press **Join as second front desk**.
4. Going on a break? Your shift stays open when you sign out. A colleague can take over the computer with their PIN (your name, top right → **Switch user**).

## Check a guest in
1. **Room board** → a room marked **Vacant** → **Check in**.
2. Pick the length of stay (6, 12, 18, 24 hours, overnight…). The price comes from the rate table; you cannot type it.
3. Type the guest's **Full name** (mobile and plate number are optional). Pick a discount only if the guest qualifies.
4. Under "Cash received" type what the guest gave you. The change and the key deposit are worked out for you.
5. **Check in and print slip.** Give the slip and the key to the guest.
- "Room just taken" means a colleague or the kiosk got the room first. Nothing was saved; pick the room it offers you.

## Sell a drink or a snack
- **Walk-in guest:** **POS** → tap the items → **Walk-in · cash** → type the cash received → **Complete sale**.
- **Guest in a room:** **POS** → tap the items → **Charge to room** → pick the room → **Complete sale**. It is added unpaid to the guest's folio. Or open the room and use **Add charge**.

## Extend, pay, move
- Open the room from the board: **Extend** (more hours), **Take payment** (pay what is unpaid), **Transfer** (another room, needs a supervisor PIN).

## Check a guest out
1. Open the room → **Check out**.
2. Answer the questions about the key, the remote and any damage → **Next: settle**.
3. Take any balance due. The key deposit is handed back to the guest unless something was lost.
4. **Check out guest.** The room goes to housekeeping.

## Close your shift and hand over
1. **Close shift** (top bar) → step **Check**: open any folio it lists, then **Next: count cash**.
2. **Count cash:** type the pieces of each bill and the coins. You see what was expected only after **Confirm count**. You cannot change the count after that.
3. **Result:** if you are short or over, pick a reason and write what happened → **Next: hand over**.
4. **Handover:** the next front desk picks their name, counts the drawer themselves and types it, enters their PIN, then **Accept and open … shift**. If their count is different they use **Amount is different — report it**.
5. Print the shift slip if asked. Sign out.

## When something goes wrong
- **Red bar "No connection to the server":** do not take payments or press Save again. Wait until the bar disappears.
- **"Something went wrong. Nothing was saved."** Try once more. If it happens again, write down the request id shown (like `7F3K-2Q9A`) and tell your admin.
- **"There is not that much cash in the drawer":** the expense is more than the drawer holds. Ask your supervisor.
- **Ten wrong PINs** lock PIN entry on this computer. A supervisor unlocks it with their password.
