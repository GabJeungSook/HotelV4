# Back office — quick guide

One page. Words in **bold** are the buttons and menu items on the screen.

## Every morning
1. Sign in. **Dashboard** shows today's sales, rooms, the open shift and **Needs attention**. Every row in Needs attention opens the screen that fixes it.
2. **Shift reports**: open each shift closed since yesterday. Check the short or over and the reason the front desk gave.
3. **Z-report**: the paper of the last closed shift. **Export** gives the PDF (A4) and the CSV; printing is logged and a second print says REPRINT.

## Close yesterday
1. **Daily sales** → pick the date. One column per shift, the day total last. The check under the table must be green: the shifts agree with the ledger.
2. If it does: **Close the day**. You get the day Z (38b) with its own number.
3. The day cannot be closed while a shift of that date is still open, out of order, or when the check found a difference. The screen says which; fix that first (usually a shift someone forgot to close).

## Other reports
- **Occupancy**: guests at the start, in, out and at the end of the day; they always add up.
- **Housekeeping**: rooms cleaned, average time, late cleans.
- **Inventory**: opening, received, sold, wasted and closing stock per product.
- **Audit trail**: who did what — voids, cancellations, approvals, price changes, sign-ins.
- **Stats**: longer periods, by hour, day or month. **Export** gives a CSV.
- Every report has **Export** for CSV; reports 37–43 also print on A4.

## Rules that do not change
- Money rows are never edited or deleted. A mistake is corrected with a void or a reversing row, with a reason, and both stay on the reports.
- A closed shift's figures never change. A later correction shows on the shift in which it was made.
- Short and over are shown separately and never cancel each other out.

## When something goes wrong
- **"Something went wrong. Nothing was saved."** Try once more. If it happens again, give the request id to your admin; it is in the log with the time and your name.
- Figures on the dashboard look old: they refresh every 30 seconds; the time of the last refresh is at the top. Closed reports never depend on the dashboard.
